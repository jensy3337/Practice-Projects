      Indian Stock Market Basics

      What is Stock Market (India)?

      Indian stock market is where shares of publicly listed Indian companies are traded on exchanges like NSE and BSE.

      NSE vs BSE

      NSE (National Stock Exchange) is known for high liquidity and NIFTY 50 index; BSE (Bombay Stock Exchange) is Asia's oldest exchange and tracks SENSEX.

      NIFTY & SENSEX

      NIFTY 50 tracks 50 large NSE firms; SENSEX tracks 30 major BSE companies.

      Bull vs Bear Market

      Bull market means rising prices and optimism; Bear market means falling prices and caution.

      Candlestick Patterns

      Common examples: Doji (indecision), Hammer (possible reversal), Engulfing (trend reversal signal).

      Intraday vs Delivery

      Intraday: buy/sell same day. Delivery: hold stocks beyond market close.

      Market Order vs Limit Order

      Market order executes immediately at best available price. Limit order executes only at your specified price.

      Risk Management Basics

      Use stop-loss, position sizing, diversification, and avoid overleveraging.
